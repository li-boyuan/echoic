# Echoic - Facebook Ad Copy

## Funnel Positioning

Do not sell this as a free AI toy. Sell it as a low-cost way for authors with finished manuscripts to create a downloadable audiobook.

- Primary offer: "Free sample, full audiobook for $9.99"
- Best audience: self-published authors who already have a manuscript, back catalog, newsletter, Gumroad/Shopify store, or KDP presence
- Avoid: broad "writers," "book lovers," and generic AI-tool audiences
- Landing URL for cold ads: `https://echoic.studio`
- Landing URL for retargeting: `https://echoic.studio/pricing`

- Preview generators
- Uploaders
- 30-day website visitors

## Event Optimization

- Early spend: optimize for `ViewContent` on pricing or studio visits until there is enough volume.
- After checkout volume exists: optimize for `InitiateCheckout`.
- Once purchase volume is stable: optimize for `Purchase`.
- Do not optimize cold ads for upload events. Uploads are useful leads, but they can over-optimize toward free sample users.

## Budget

- Start with $10-20/day per test for 3-5 days.
- Kill ads with clicks but no pricing/studio engagement.
- Retarget visitors separately with a purchase-specific message.
